Provider wallet

The seed is encrypted at rest and unwrapped automatically on restart. Generating a new one stops the old wallet being used — move its funds out first.

Write this down now

Shown once, never again. Anyone holding this phrase controls the provider's funds. Store it in your password manager or on paper — not in a chat, an email or a screenshot.

Deposit SCRT

Sends native SCRT from your own wallet to the provider. Keplr shows you the destination and the amount and asks you to approve — nothing is signed here.

Enough to sponsor gas for a while. You can always top up later.

Wrap SCRT into sSCRT

Calibration pays itself in sSCRT, so the provider needs some before it can measure anything. This is a real transaction and costs gas.

Calibrate payment gas

Sends real self-transfers and reads back the gas each one used. It cannot be simulated — Secret will not simulate an encrypted contract call. Expect a few samples to be lost to the public endpoint; the run needs at least three to record anything.

Roughly 0.02 SCRT of gas per sample at the current price.

Calibrate contract gas

Same measurement as the payment message, for one whitelisted contract. The call runs as the provider's own account, so it has to be one that would actually succeed for that address — and it should be shaped like the most expensive call you intend to sponsor, because the single recorded constant has to cover all of them.
