Provider wallet

It needs native SCRT: every credit sold is paid into the vault out of this balance, and a cold buyer's first transaction is paid for by a small allowance from it.

Stored encrypted under your admin password, so a copy of the database file alone does not yield it.

Write this down

Shown once and never again. It is the only way back into this wallet, and anyone who has it can spend everything in it.

Wrap SCRT into sSCRT

Deposits native SCRT into the sSCRT contract, 1:1 and reversible. The only reason to do it is calibration, which measures with real self-transfers and so needs a small balance to move around.

One is plenty; calibration moves fractions of a uscrt at a time.

Calibrate payment gas

Sends real self-transfers and reads back the gas each used. It cannot be simulated — Secret will not simulate an encrypted contract call. Expect a sample or two to be lost to the public endpoint; at least three have to succeed.

Three is enough. The recorded constant is the highest sample plus 15 %, not an average, and this message has never varied by more than four gas — so more samples cannot make it more accurate, only find a higher maximum and make every quote dearer.

Settings

Where credits are bought from. Empty stops sales. Check it before saving: the vault has no withdrawal, so SCRT paid to the wrong address does not come back.
What one purchase buys, and the ceiling on what a client may ask for. Paid into the vault out of the provider's own SCRT, so it also caps how fast one caller can draw that reserve down.
The whole revenue. It has to cover the buyer's transaction and the provider's delivery transaction both, or a quote is refused rather than sold at a loss.
What an address with no gas gets so it can sign its one purchase. This is the most a buyer who never pays can cost.
Short. It covers one purchase, and an unused one is left to expire because revoking it costs more than it reclaims.
Redeems accumulated sSCRT back into native SCRT. Without it the reserve credits are bought from only ever shrinks.
